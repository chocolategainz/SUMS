What is SUMS?

SUMS brings together the different areas of a Students' Union that are normally managed separately. Instead of relying on multiple systems, spreadsheets, and processes, SUMS connects these areas into one central platform. This gives teams a clearer view of key information, helping them understand student engagement and make more informed decisions.
